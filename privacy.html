Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

QuickTools is a collection of free browser-based utilities. All tools run entirely in your browser — no data you enter (text, images, files) is transmitted to any server. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Text you type or paste into any tool
  • Images or files you upload to the Image Compressor
  • Passwords generated using the Password Generator
  • Any form of account or profile data

Cookies and Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show you personalised ads.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, visit: google.com/policies/privacy/partners.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may collect basic server logs including IP addresses and browser user-agent strings. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Local Storage

This site saves your last-used tool and theme preference in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser data.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
